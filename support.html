Subscriptions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Soulful, and choose Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Premium stays open until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I avoid being charged after a free trial?

Cancel in the same place at least 24 hours before the trial ends. The paywall shows the exact date. If you cancel in time, you pay nothing.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Open Soulful, go to the Premium screen and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Can I keep using Soulful for free?

Yes. "Continue with the free plan" is always on the Premium screen. The free plan includes a limited number of prayers each week and the free recordings.

Your data

How do I delete my account?

In Soulful open Profile → Settings and choose the delete option at the bottom. Your prayers, Noor messages and profile are deleted at once. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Does AI read my words?

Only when you ask for a prayer or talk with Noor, and only after you allow AI processing. You can withdraw it in Settings → Privacy. Words that read as a crisis are never sent. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I ask for a copy of my data, or use my GDPR, CCPA or KVKK rights?

Write to truespanmedia@gmail.com from the email linked to your account, or include your account details from Profile → Settings. We answer within 30 days.

Using Soulful

How do I change my tradition?

Open Profile → Prayer preferences → Tradition. Soulful asks before it changes its look and the prayers it shows.

Are the prayers quotes from scripture?

No. Prayers are written for you by AI and are not scripture or religious rulings. See the Terms of Use.

Is Soulful a crisis service?

No. If you are in danger or crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line now. You can find one near you at findahelpline.com.
