Soulful

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-13 · Contact: truespanmedia@gmail.com

Soulful is a prayer app. This page says, in plain words, what we collect, why, who processes it for us, how long we keep it and how you delete it. "We" means Soulful.

What we collect

  • Your account. An account ID created when you first open the app. If you choose to sign in, your email address or your Apple or Google account ID.
  • Your choices. The first name you enter, your tradition, your language and the answers you pick when you start (what brings you here, your hardest hour). Your tradition is a religious belief, which is sensitive information: we use it only to shape your prayers and the look of the app.
  • Your words. Your notes, the prayers you create or save, and your messages to Noor and its replies.
  • Your permissions and limits. Whether you allowed your words to be sent to the writing service, how many prayers and Noor replies you used this period, and whether Premium is active. We never see your card details.

Soulful has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and does no tracking. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID. Reminders are set on your phone; which reminder types are on is saved to your Soulful account. We never sell your data.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and keep your prayers and conversations.
  • To write a prayer or a Noor reply, only after you allow it and only when you ask.
  • To show your tradition's prayers and recordings.
  • To apply fair-use limits, keep the service secure, unlock Premium and answer you.

Words that read as a crisis are never sent to the AI or saved as a prayer: the app shows help lines on your phone instead.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase stores the database and runs sign-in and our server functions.
  • Google (Gemini models) writes prayers and Noor replies. A request goes first through OpenRouter (United States), which passes it to Google; if OpenRouter cannot answer, the same request is sent directly to Google. They receive only what that request needs (your tradition and words; for Noor, your message, first name and your last few messages), only after you allow it. We ask OpenRouter to use only providers that do not store requests or train on them; each provider handles requests under its own terms.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions and receives your account ID and purchase receipts.
  • Apple handles App Store payments and Sign in with Apple. Google handles Google sign-in if you choose it.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries under their own data protection terms.

How long we keep it

  • While your account exists. You can delete a single prayer, or clear your Noor conversation, at any time.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Settings). Your data is deleted at once. We keep a short confirmation record for 30 days so an interrupted deletion can finish; it holds no name, email, prayer or account ID.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel it in your Apple ID settings. Apple and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records.
  • Copies cached on your phone stay until you delete the app or your account.

Children

Soulful is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or withdraw a permission at any time (Settings → Privacy). If you live in the EU or UK (GDPR), California (CCPA) or Türkiye (KVKK), these are also your legal rights: we use your account data to provide the service you asked for, and your tradition and the words you send to the writing service on your explicit consent, which you can withdraw. We do not sell or share personal information. Write to truespanmedia@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Security and changes

Data travels over encrypted connections, and each account can reach only its own rows. We will update this page and its date when our practices change.
